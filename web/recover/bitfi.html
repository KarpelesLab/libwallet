libwallet

Recover a Bitfi wallet

Enter the salt and secret phrase you use on your Bitfi device to see the funds they control and move them. There's nothing to install and no account. This tool isn't made by or affiliated with Bitfi.

Nothing is saved. Your salt and secret are used on this page and never sent anywhere. Closing this window forgets them.

Move what you find to a new wallet. A Bitfi secret can't be changed, and typing it on a computer exposes it to anything running there, including browser extensions.

Balances come from our server. To check them, this page sends your addresses to rpc.modchain.net, which can see that they're being looked up together.
